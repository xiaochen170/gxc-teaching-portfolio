Self-Reflection Video

A plan for a mid-semester reflection on my teaching experiences, professional growth, and goals as a language educator.

省
Planned · Fall 2026

Reflection video — coming soon

I plan to record this reflection at mid-semester, drawing on experiences from my CHI 101 classes. The video will be added here when it is ready.

01 What the video will address

Looking back, looking forward

The video will be a candid, first-person account rather than a polished lecture. I plan to organize it around four questions:

1. Where I started

How a background in Chinese literature and dialectology prepared me — and did not prepare me — for standing in front of a room of absolute beginners. What I expected from CHI 101 in August, and what surprised me.

2. What I have learned from my students

I will select specific classroom moments and consider what they reveal about learning: which activities supported participation, where students needed more guidance, and how their questions informed my teaching.

3. How my practice has changed

Adjustments to pacing, error correction, the balance of pinyin and characters, and the ways I check understanding. The relationship between what I wrote in my teaching philosophy and what actually happens in class.

4. Goals for the coming year

What I want to develop next as a teacher: better formative assessment, more culturally rich materials, and more confident classroom management in a new academic culture. Longer-term, how teaching Chinese shapes my research on language, literature, and identity — and vice versa.

Questions for reflection after class

  • Who spoke today?Did every student produce Chinese aloud, and to whom?
  • What did I over-explain?Where could a demonstration have replaced a paragraph?
  • What surprised me?A question, an error, a moment of laughter — and what it tells me about how students are learning.
  • What changes next time?What is one concrete adjustment I can make in the next lesson?

Format

  • LengthAbout 5–8 minutes
  • LanguageEnglish, with Chinese examples
