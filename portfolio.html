01 The course

CHI 101 · Elementary Chinese I

I teach two sections of ASU's first-semester Mandarin course for students with no prior background. The course builds the foundations of pronunciation and tones, pinyin, basic characters, and everyday communication — greetings, names, family, dates and time, hobbies, and daily life — with culture woven throughout.

03 A first look

Proposed activity: Name-card exchange

A draft activity for the first weeks of CHI 101. This proposal illustrates a communicative task; classroom materials and reflections will be added after implementation.

標
Objective

Students can ask for and give a name, and greet a new acquaintance, using 你好 / 我叫… / 你叫什么名字？/ 认识你很高兴, with attention to the tones of each phrase.

序
Procedure

Model the exchange with one student (2 min) → choral tone practice (3 min) → students write their Chinese name on a card, then circulate and complete the exchange with at least five classmates, collecting signatures (10 min) → report back: “他叫…” (5 min).

效
Teaching rationale

The task is designed to give learners repeated practice with different partners. A name card can support recall, while a brief report-back offers an opportunity to introduce third-person forms and check understanding.
