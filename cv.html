03 Selected research
01
2025 · NTU Singapore

The Cyborg City and Human Alienation in Hong Kong Literature

Supervisor: Prof. Yow Cheun Hoe, Nanyang Technological University

Reads Hon Lai Chu, Dorothy Tse, and Dung Kai-cheung as responses to the posthuman condition under techno-capitalism — Hong Kong as a vast “cyborg” city. Prepared for submission to the 2026 Biennial Conference of Chinese and Comparative Literature.

02
2025 · HKU

The Ru Tone in Yangzhou Dialect: Diachronic, Synchronic, Empirical

Supervisor: Prof. Richard Van Ness Simmons

Compares performances of “Wu Song Fights the Tiger” by Yangzhou pinghua master Wang Shaotang (1889–1968) and fifth-generation inheritor Ma Wei to trace sound change, with implications for revitalizing endangered dialects.

03
2024 — 2025 · HKU · Research project

Diaspora and Belonging: Ge Liang's Hong Kong Writing through Spatial Narrative

Supervisor: Prof. Wei Yan

Integrates diaspora literature, narratology, spatial theory, and postcolonial theory to examine how Food is Heaven constructs belonging through space.

04
2024 — 2025 · HKU

Postcolonial Identity Writing in The Exorcist: Body, Space, and Time

Supervisor: Prof. Leung Suk Man

How Shi Shuqing reveals the identity paradox of postcolonial Hong Kong through colonial legacy, cultural hybridity, and “ghost writing.”

05
2022 — 2023 · NJU

Urban Language Survey: Language Competence in the AI Era

Supervisor: Prof. Wang Ling, China Language Strategy Research Center

Questionnaire design and semi-structured interviews; three survey rounds yielded 176 valid samples linking urban language environments, family language planning, and dialect inheritance.

06
2021 — 2022 · NJU · National Innovation Program

American Literature in the Historical Context of Marxist Dissemination

Supervisor: Prof. Yang Jincai

Invited to the 4th High-End Forum on Contemporary English Literature in China (Renmin University). Three papers on Jack London, Theodore Dreiser, and John Steinbeck.

Further projects on Mo Yan, Liu Yichang, Dream of the Red Chamber, and verbal impoliteness in Border Town (26th NJU Basic Disciplines Thesis Award) are listed in the full C.V.
