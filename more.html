01 Before ASU

Where my teaching began

My interest in teaching developed through remote lessons, guided reading, and individual mentoring. These experiences continue to inform how I support learners.

2023 · Guangxi

Teach for China — remote teaching volunteer

Delivered online lessons to primary school students in rural Guangxi through an international volunteer program, learning to hold attention and build rapport through a screen.

2023 · Gansu

“Reading Together” volunteer

Completed guided reading with primary school students in Gansu through Nanjing University's program — an opportunity to connect my interest in literature with support for young readers.

2024 — 2025

Companion Mentor, Xinzhi Pengbei

Mentored adolescents over eighteen months, supporting their academic and personal development — sustained, individual attention that now shapes how I look at each student in my sections.
